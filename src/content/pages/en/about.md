Our journey began in 2018, guided by the vision of Rajeev Agarwal to partner with the leading eyecare systems to expand eyecare services. India Eye Institute | इंडिया आई हॉस्पिटल is a social enterprise and is set up in collaboration with Aravind Eye Care System.

![India Eye Institute hospital](/images/indiaeye/about-hospital.png)

## We exist to provide

high quality, advanced eye treatment

**Vision:** To eliminate needless blindness

**Mission:** To provide high quality eye care services to one million patients every year

![Eye care at India Eye Institute](/images/indiaeye/about-eye-care.jpg)

Our National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers (NABH) certification signifies our commitment to high-quality patient care and safety.

We are working with LAICO - Aravind Eye Care System, Madurai and LV Prasad Eye Research Center, Hyderabad for clinical and operational expertise. These institutions are the largest eye care systems in the world.

## Social Impact

Our accomplished and experienced surgeons conduct at least 10 surgeries with great results everyday. Over the last year, we have conducted over 1,400 cataract surgeries.
