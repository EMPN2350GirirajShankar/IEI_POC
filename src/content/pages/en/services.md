## Cataract

Cataracts are a common eye problem that usually happens as people get older. They can also be caused by other things like certain diseases, eye injuries, too much sun, or poor diet. Signs of cataracts include blurry vision, trouble seeing colors, sensitivity to bright lights at night, and difficulty reading. Surgery to remove cataracts is recommended when they start to make it hard to do everyday activities, not just when they're fully developed.

![Cataract eye care](/images/indiaeye/services-cataract.jpg)

### Facts

- Development Age: Cataracts usually start developing in the 40s or 50s.
- Vision Impact: Begin to affect vision around the age of 60.
- Consequences of Untreated Cataracts: Can worsen over time and may lead to complete vision loss if not treated.

### Symptoms

If you observe these symptoms, consult with a doctor:

- Blurred or clouded vision
- Seeing halos around lights
- Problem with night vision
- Problem with bright lights
- Fading or yellowing of colors
- Double vision

The purpose of cataract treatment is to remove or dissolve cataracts from the outer eye lens.

### Types of Cataract Surgery

**Phacoemulsification (PHACO)**

The most common cataract surgery today is called phacoemulsification (PHACO). It uses foldable Acryfold Lens (IOLs). The cloudy lens is removed using an ultrasonic probe. The folded lens implant is inserted and then unfolds inside the eye.

**Extracapsular**

Conventional cataract surgery removes the cloudy lens but keeps the back capsule. It requires a 12-mm cut that is closed with stitches. Small incision cataract surgery uses a smaller 6-mm cut. This smaller cut usually doesn’t need stitches because it seals itself.

**Intracapsular**

This is a rare type of cataract surgery. The entire eye lens and its capsule are removed, and a new artificial lens (IOL) is put in its place. A large cut is needed for the surgery and there is a higher chance of problems or complications.
